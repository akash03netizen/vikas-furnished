Our Story

Furniture That Feels Like Home

Vikas Furnished began with a simple belief — that every home deserves furniture that is beautiful, durable, and thoughtfully designed. What started as a small workshop has grown into one of India's trusted premium furniture brands.

Our journey is rooted in a deep respect for craftsmanship. Every sofa, bed, and dining set we create passes through the hands of skilled artisans who bring decades of experience to their work.

Today, we offer a complete range of premium furniture — from sofas and beds to dining sets, wardrobes, and office desks. Each piece is crafted with sustainably sourced materials and built to last for generations.
